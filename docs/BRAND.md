# Pennovia — Brand

**Descriptor:** Open-source author voice toolkit  
**Tagline:** Your voice. Your words.

Pennovia analyzes writing style and guides drafts. **Core** is the Python integration layer; **CLI** supports automation; **Studio** is the local writing workspace. It is not a hosted service.

## Voice

Plain, precise English. Describe verified behavior, not aspirations. Never promise authentic authorship, preserved memories or guaranteed output quality.

## Visual identity

Studio's current palette in `pennovia/studio/app.css`: ink `#282521`, canvas `#f7f6f2`, paper `#fffcf8`, accent `#a94f38`. Readable typography and restrained color take priority over decoration.

## Terms

- **Voice profile:** portable, local style statistics.
- **Draft:** model output requiring editorial review.
- **Voice review:** heuristic comparison, not an identity test.
- **Export:** user-initiated output; Studio sends WordPress drafts only.

Keep the product independent of unrelated brands, tools and proprietary runtime requirements.
